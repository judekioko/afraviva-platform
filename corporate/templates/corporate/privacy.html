5. Photos, video and children

We feature real people in our stories, events, farms and homes. We publish an identifiable person's photo, video, voice or story only with their consent.

  • Adults sign a photo and video release before we publish, naming where the content may appear.
  • Children under 18 appear only with written consent from a parent or guardian (section 33 of the Data Protection Act). We do not publish a child's full name, school or location alongside their image.
  • Crowd and event shots are taken at events announced as filmed. Anyone who asks will be blurred or removed.
  • Withdrawing consent: anyone featured can ask us to remove their image or story by contacting us (section 1). We remove it from our websites within 14 days and stop using it in new material.

6. How long we keep data and how we protect it

We keep personal data only for the periods in section 2. Enquiries, staff access requests and admin login records are deleted automatically once their period ends. A full backup of the sites is taken weekly and only the four most recent are kept, so deleted data leaves our backups within about one month.

  • All sites use HTTPS encryption.
  • Admin access is limited to named staff, locks after repeated failed logins and times out when idle.
  • Passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form.
  • Enquiries are visible only to staff who handle them.
  • Location and camera details are removed from photos when they are uploaded.
  • Our fonts and images are served from our own server, so viewing a page does not share your IP address with font or image providers.

7. Your rights

You can contact us (section 1) to exercise these rights under the Data Protection Act, 2019:

  • Be informed of how your data is used (this policy).
  • Access a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Delete your data, or object to our using it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.
  • Data portability: receive your data in a common format, or have it sent to another organisation.

We confirm requests within 7 days and complete them as soon as possible, free of charge. We may ask for proof of identity first.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya, or, for our Uganda farm work, to Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office.
