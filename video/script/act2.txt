# ACT II - THE FAMILY THAT SAYS YES
# Lines starting with '#' are ignored. Lines in (parentheses) are delivery cues: they are not spoken,
# they set the mood of the voice and the music (warm | serious | rising | majestic).
@warm
But the Incarnation required the YES of a man as well.

Joseph. A just man, who could have chosen his reputation, his comfort and his own plans.

Instead, he obeyed. He took Mary as his wife. He protected her dignity. He worked with his hands. He fled to Egypt to save the Child, and he returned when God called him home.

His strength was not domination. His strength was protection.

@serious
Yet look around us. Fathers consumed by ambition. Marriages broken by selfishness and infidelity. Children growing up without their fathers. Women whose homes have become places of fear.

And we must speak plainly.

Domestic violence is not authority. Rape is not masculinity. Abuse is not discipline. Cruelty is not strength.

For Christ does not abuse His Bride. He gives Himself for her.

No woman should be told that enduring abuse is her Christian duty. No child should be taught that violence is love.

@rising
The answer is the same YES that Joseph and Mary gave.

It is a thousand little YESes. YES to life. YES to fidelity. YES to forgiveness. YES to prayer. YES to responsibility. And sometimes, when the Cross comes, YES to suffering united with Christ.

For the Resurrection, not suffering, is the final word.

@majestic
At Fatima, Our Lady promised: in the end, my Immaculate Heart will triumph.

But this triumph is not only the defeat of some earthly power. It is grace over sin, truth over error, love over hatred, obedience over rebellion.

And it begins in the human heart. In the marriage restored. In the father who returns to his vocation. In the mother whose dignity is honoured. In the child who grows up knowing God.

The family is the first school of virtue, and the first place a child learns to know God. A society cannot be healthy while its families collapse.

So the triumph of Our Lady is the triumph of the family that says YES.

The Holy Family of Nazareth remains our model: Mary, the perfect mother. Joseph, the faithful guardian. Jesus, the Son of God.

Not through fear, but through faith.
Not through domination, but through charity.
Not through the glorification of man, but through the glorification of God.
